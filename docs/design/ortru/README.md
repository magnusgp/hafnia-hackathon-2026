# Ortru design record

Two operator-workspace compositions were drawn against the real data shape (`approach-a-dock.html`, `approach-b-stage.html`;
open them in a browser) and compared at 1440 by 900.

- **A, Dock.** Queue rail, dominant investigation, evidence and decision in a right panel. Queue scans fast and keeps context;
  the decision is far from the footage, and the picture is capped by the panel widths.
- **B, Stage.** A horizontal strip of exceptions above a wide picture with the decision bar docked directly under it. The camera
  gets the most space and the Send action sits beside the footage; the strip scales badly beyond a handful of exceptions.

**Chosen: A with B's docked decision bar.** The queue stays a vertical rail (many exceptions, reasons readable at a glance),
the investigation is the wide column, and the question, answer options and the XProtect action sit directly under the footage.
The right column carries only the two sources and what each established.

Identity: `brand-kit/ortru/` (editable SVG mark, single-colour mark, favicon, wordmark). The left head is Hafnia (Polar blue),
the right head is independent local vision (violet); the same two colours mark the two sources everywhere in the interface.
`mark-sheet.html` shows the mark at 160 to 16 px on light and dark.
